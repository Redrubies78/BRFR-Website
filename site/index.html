The perfect getaway

Your Escape. Our Farmhouse.

Wake up to grazing cows, open pastures and panoramic mountain views from the wraparound porch. Blue Ridge Farmhouse Retreat is a fully renovated 1920s farmhouse in Mount Jackson, Virginia, built for slowing down and reconnecting.

Spend your days by the private pool, unwind in the aromatherapy barrel sauna, and end the night in the hot tub under the Milky Way. Wineries are minutes away, Bryce Resort is just down the road, and Shenandoah National Park is close enough for a morning hike.

It's ideal for romantic weekends, girls' and guys' reset trips, summer family vacations, mountain biking adventures and cozy winter ski escapes.

Unwind. Reconnect. Repeat.

Before you go

Questions

Why book direct instead of Airbnb or Vrbo?

Booking here skips the platform service fees, and you deal directly with us from the first question to check-out.

What are check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is 3:00 PM and check-out is 10:00 AM. Self check-in uses a smart lock, and your code is sent before arrival.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes. One pet is welcome per stay, and the backyard is fully fenced so pups can run and play.

Is the pool heated?

The pool is not heated. It's at its best during the warm months. The hot tub and sauna are ready all year.

How many people does the farmhouse sleep?

Up to 6 guests across 3 bedrooms (king, queen, and a double + twin bunk room), with 2 full bathrooms. The primary renter must be at least 25.

Anything else we should know?

This is a true 1920s farmhouse with low ceilings and doorways full of character, so taller guests should mind their heads. The home has stairs and is not wheelchair accessible. There is an exterior camera at the entrance only. The farmhouse sits on a quiet country road beside a working farm.

What is there to do nearby?

Bryce Resort is about 8 miles away for skiing, golf and mountain biking. Shenandoah National Park is about 19 miles away and Skyline Drive about 21. Wineries and breweries are minutes away. See our Hiking & Wineries guide.
